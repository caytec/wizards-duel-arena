Wizards' Duel Arena is an exciting multiplayer game where players engage in real-time spell-casting battles, using a variety of spells to defeat their opponents. Join the arena to prove your wizarding skills and become the ultimate champion! This engaging game offers spell selection, dynamic combat, and strategic positioning in beautifully designed arenas with unique weather effects.

### Key Features
- Multiplayer battles in real time
- Spell casting with unique mechanics
- Various arenas with dynamic weather
- Progression system with experience and leveling
- Unlock new spells and cosmetic upgrades

### Instructions
Players will enter the arena, select their spells, and use WASD for movement, aiming and casting spells through number keys. Dodge attacks with Shift and enjoy unique spells that enhance gameplay. Join now and prove your magical prowess!
